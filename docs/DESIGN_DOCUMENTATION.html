🏛️ Sahayak AI (सहायक AI)

Government Welfare Scheme Advisor & Explainable Match Engine

Team: Analog Roses  |  Event: Hack for Social Cause  |  Date: October 2026

Executive Summary: Sahayak AI is a privacy-first digital public service discovery tool that eliminates welfare exclusion by indexing 2,066 Indian Central and State government schemes over an explainable 7-dimension scoring engine. It collects zero personally identifiable information (no Aadhaar, no bank accounts) and transparently reveals why each scheme was recommended before directing the citizen to official portals.

1. Problem Space & Need Analysis

  • Information Fragmentation: Welfare policies are spread across dozens of departmental silos with varying guidelines.
  • Exclusion Errors: Millions of eligible citizens never apply because eligibility rules are perceived as opaque.
  • Data Privacy Hesitation: Asking citizens for Aadhaar or bank passbooks creates apprehension and fraud risk.
  • Hallucination Hazards: Unconstrained generative AI models frequently invent false grants or incorrect eligibility criteria.

3. The 7-Factor Scoring Matrix

Dimension Weight Description & Method
Needs Relevance 35% TF-IDF vector text similarity between stated citizen support needs and scheme descriptions/tags.
State Coverage Fit 20% 100% for state matches; 85% for national Central schemes; excludes schemes of other states.
Age Boundary Fit 15% Validates numerical age brackets and seniority criteria against citizen age.
Income Fit 10% Matches BPL / low-income brackets with government subsidy rules.
Gender Focus 10% Prioritizes women and girl-child specific programs when beneficiary is female.
Social Category 5% Detects SC, ST, OBC, or minority priority criteria.
Residence Fit 5% Tailors recommendations to Rural vs. Urban targeted programs.

5. Privacy, Security & Disclaimers

  • Zero PII Intake: Never requires Aadhaar, phone numbers, bank accounts, or documents.
  • Truth Anchoring: All recommendations are preliminary; users are directed to official portal links (e.g. myscheme.gov.in, pmkisan.gov.in).
  • Deterministic Matcher: Avoids generative AI hallucinations by relying on explainable TF-IDF text metrics and objective rules.
